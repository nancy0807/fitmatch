# Personas

## Primary persona

Online apparel shopper, has been burned by cross-brand sizing enough times to change behavior around it.

**Trigger type:** brand unfamiliarity, not price or style uncertainty. The hesitation isn't "will I like this," it's "will this fit" — and it surfaces specifically at the moment of selecting a size on an unfamiliar product page.

**Current coping behavior:** over-ordering multiple sizes to return the wrong ones, reading reviews for fit comments, or avoiding the brand entirely rather than risk a bad guess. All three are lossy — money, time, or a purchase that never happens.

**Job to be done:** *"When I want to buy from a brand I haven't worn before, I want to know what size to order, so I can buy with confidence instead of guessing, over-ordering, or avoiding the purchase."*
