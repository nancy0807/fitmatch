# Opportunity Spaces

Where FitMatch intervenes, and why.

**The intervention point is Moment 3** — the size selector on an unfamiliar brand's product page. It's the last moment before a costly guess gets made, and it's the only moment where a prediction can still change the outcome.

**Why AI, specifically, at this point:** a static size-chart lookup only works if the shopper already knows their exact body measurements — most don't, and asking them to measure themselves adds enough friction to kill adoption before it starts. The harder, AI-relevant problem is translating subjective fit feedback ("snug in the shoulders, roomy at the waist") across brands with different cuts into a usable prediction. That's pattern inference over noisy signal, not a database join.

**What a plain rule-based system can't do:** hand-coded if/else logic per brand doesn't generalize — every new brand pair needs its own rule, and rules don't improve as more fit data accumulates. Inference from fit history does.
