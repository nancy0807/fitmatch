Market Analysis

The scale of the problem, and why it's still open.

Key findings
. Size/fit is the #1 cited reason for online apparel returns — 53% of surveyed US apparel decision-makers name it, ahead of colour (16%) and damage (10%) (Coresight Research, The True Cost of Apparel Returns, 2023)
. 24.4% average online apparel return rate — $38B in returned merchandise, $25.1B in processing costs alone, against a $155.8B US online apparel/footwear market (Coresight, 2023)
. 25% e-commerce apparel return rate independently confirmed by McKinsey's Returns Management Survey, vs. 20% for retail overall
. The industry has already thrown money at this: 85% of apparel retailers are implementing or planning virtual try-on; 29% already run a size-recommender tool, of which 80% report a conversion lift (Coresight, 2023)
. Despite that investment, only 1 in 4 retailers offer clienteling or cross-brand fit guidance (McKinsey) — the industry has built size prediction within a brand, not across brands
. No universal sizing standard exists across apparel brands, and standardisation attempts have not succeeded — the root cause is structural, not a data gap that will close on its own
